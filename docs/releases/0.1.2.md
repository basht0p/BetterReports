# BetterReports 0.1.2

- Automatically restricts named-tenant reports to documents whose `organization.name` exactly matches the tenant name. Private-tenant reports use the authenticated username.
- Adds **Organization Scope** under **Report Settings** for Global-tenant users with `cluster:admin/betterreports/admin` permission. Select a named tenant to restrict the report, or select **Global** and acknowledge the multi-customer warning before saving.
- Enforces organization scope in the OpenSearch companion for previews, manual generation, and scheduled execution. Scope is separate from editable report filters.
- Left-aligns checkboxes and their labels throughout the report builder, including Hide legend and the footer options.

Compatible with OpenSearch and OpenSearch Dashboards 3.8.0. Upgrade both plugins together and hard-refresh the browser. Add `indices:admin/mappings/get` to report users' existing source-index permissions, preserve their DLS/FLS restrictions, and save/reauthorize older reports. Review [Deployment](../DEPLOYMENT.md) for scope permissions, field mappings, and existing-report migration.
